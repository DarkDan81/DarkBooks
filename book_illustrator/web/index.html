A short preface
Your first illustrated book.
A few simple steps, then let the story unfold.
I.
Choose your models
Connect a text model to read the book and an image model to illustrate it. Local and cloud connections can be mixed.
II.
Open a book
Add an FB2 file to your library. Choose the chapters, describe the atmosphere, and pick an illustration style.
III.
Try a chapter
Make a sample edition first. Once you like the result, return to the original book and illustrate the chapters you want.
IV.
Take it with you
Download the illustrated FB2 for your reader, or save the images separately. Pause whenever you need to and resume later.
